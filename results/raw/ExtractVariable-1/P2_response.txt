There are extract variable refactoring opportunities in Checker. The clearest example is the repeated call to CommonUtil.relativizeAndNormalizePath(basedir, fileName) in acceptFileStarted, fireFileStarted, fireErrors, and fireFileFinished.
However, each call occurs in a separate method, so extracting a local variable would not eliminate duplication across methods. Within individual methods, there are no obvious repeated expressions that justify an extract variable refactoring.
No extract variable refactoring is necessary.
