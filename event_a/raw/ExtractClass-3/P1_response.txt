### Important consideration

The refactoring above retains the original test structure, but there is one concurrency detail worth highlighting: the `threadStarted` latch only confirms that the worker thread has begun executing. It does not guarantee that `initializer.get()` has started waiting before the interrupt occurs.

For a completely deterministic interruption test, introduce an additional synchronization mechanism to ensure the worker has reached the relevant waiting state before interrupting it.
